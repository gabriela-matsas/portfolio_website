About Me

Front-end Developer | Developer Education | Web Technologies

I hold a degree in Music from the University of São Paulo (USP) and a Master’s degree in Music Education from the University of Minho. During my academic journey, I developed a strong interest in education, communication, and the impact of technology on learning experiences.

While writing my internship report about the influence of technology in education, I became increasingly interested in the tech field and how digital tools can transform the way people learn, interact, and access information. That experience motivated me to transition into technology and begin my journey in software development.

Currently, I am focused on building strong foundations in HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript (ES6+), and React, while developing projects that help me better understand responsive design, accessibility, component-based architecture, and modern front-end development practices.

Alongside coding, I am also interested in Developer Education, technical communication, and creating learning experiences that make complex concepts easier to understand. My long-term goal is to work at the intersection of technology, education, and developer experience, helping users and developers better engage with digital products and technologies.
